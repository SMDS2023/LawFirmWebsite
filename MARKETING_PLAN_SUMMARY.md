## 🚨 COMMON PITFALLS TO AVOID

### Don't:
1. **Publish inconsistently** - Better to post less frequently but consistently
2. **Ignore comments/engagement** - Social media is a conversation, not a broadcast
3. **Write for search engines only** - Write for humans first, optimize for SEO second
4. **Forget CTAs** - Every piece of content should have a next step
5. **Copy competitors** - Lean into YOUR unique angle (data + law enforcement background)
6. **Batch and forget** - Even scheduled content needs monitoring
7. **Skip analytics** - Data drives decisions
8. **Over-promote** - 80% value, 20% promotion rule
9. **Use legal jargon** - Explain in plain English
10. **Give up too soon** - Content marketing takes 6-12 months to see full ROI

### Do:
1. **Be consistent** - Show up daily on social, weekly on blog
2. **Engage authentically** - Be human, be helpful, be yourself
3. **Provide value first** - Educate before you sell
4. **Track everything** - What gets measured gets managed
5. **Leverage your uniqueness** - Former FHP/OCSO, data-driven, police academy instructor
6. **Repurpose content** - One blog = 15+ social posts
7. **Build email list** - You own this audience
8. **Update old content** - Keep evergreen posts current
9. **Respond quickly** - Engagement window is critical
10. **Stay patient** - Trust the process, compound growth takes time
