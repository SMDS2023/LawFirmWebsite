# Spanish Learning Curriculum - Pickleball Theme

## Overview

10-lesson progression from pickleball scoring basics to general Spanish conversation. Each lesson builds vocabulary and confidence through thematic learning anchored in familiar pickleball contexts.

---

## Lesson 1: Numbers 0-15 (Pickleball Scoring)

**Goal**: Learn basic numbers for score announcements

**Vocabulary** (12 words):
- cero, uno, dos, tres, cuatro, cinco
- seis, siete, ocho, nueve, diez, once

**Key Phrases**:
- Cero-cero, servicio (Zero-zero, serve)
- Tres-dos-uno (Score: 3-2-1)
- ¿Cuál es el marcador? (What's the score?)

**Dialogue Context**: Starting a game, announcing scores

**Grammar Focus**: Numbers, basic question formation

## Lesson 10: Daily Conversation Anchored in Pickleball

**Goal**: General conversation skills with sport as connector

**Vocabulary** (20+ phrases covering):
- Greetings: ¿Cómo estás? / Bien, gracias / ¿Y tú?
- Weather: Hace sol / Hace calor / Buen día para jugar
- Plans: ¿Qué haces hoy? / Voy a jugar / Después del partido
- Invitations: ¿Quieres venir? / Sí, me gustaría / No puedo hoy
- Location: ¿Dónde? / En la cancha / Cerca de mi casa
- Time: ¿A qué hora? / A las cinco / En una hora

**Key Phrases**:
- Hola, ¿cómo estás? Voy a jugar pickleball (Hi, how are you? I'm going to play pickleball)
- Hace buen día para estar afuera (It's a nice day to be outside)
- ¿Quieres venir a jugar con nosotros mañana? (Do you want to come play with us tomorrow?)
- Después del partido, vamos a comer (After the game, we're going to eat)

**Dialogue Context**: Full conversations that include but extend beyond pickleball

**Grammar Focus**: Review all grammar, focus on fluency and naturalness

## Lesson Structure Template

Each lesson follows this 10-15 minute structure:

1. **Vocabulary List** (8-15 words/phrases)
   - Spanish word
   - English translation
   - Audio pronunciation

2. **Example Phrases** (4-6 sentences)
   - Practical usage in context
   - Audio for each phrase

3. **Dialogue** (6-10 exchanges)
   - Natural conversation
   - Themed to lesson topic
   - Both speakers with translations

4. **Quick Quiz** (5 questions)
   - Multiple choice
   - Tests comprehension
   - Immediate feedback

5. **Review Summary** (auto-generated)
   - Words learned
   - Key phrases practiced
   - Grammar concepts covered

---

## Progression Philosophy

1. **Concrete to Abstract**: Start with numbers and visible game elements, progress to concepts and conversations
2. **Repetition with Variation**: Core verbs/phrases appear in multiple lessons with increasing complexity
3. **Functional First**: Prioritize what you'd actually say on court before grammatical perfection
4. **Confidence Building**: Early wins (counting, basic phrases) motivate continued practice
5. **Natural Transition**: Pickleball vocabulary naturally expands to sports, then daily life

---

## Post-Lesson 10: Expansion Topics

Once core 10 lessons completed, future lessons could cover:

- Food/drinks (post-game meal conversations)
- Family/friends (who you play with)
- Travel (playing pickleball in different places)
- Weather (cancellations, indoor vs outdoor)
- Equipment (discussing gear)
- Injuries/health (common in sports contexts)
- Directions (getting to courts)
- Technology (apps, scoring, video)

---

## Assessment & Iteration

**After Lessons 1-3**: Evaluate engagement, pacing, difficulty
**After Lessons 4-7**: Check retention of early vocabulary, adjust if needed
**After Lessons 8-10**: Assess readiness for voice tutor (Phase 3)

**Success Indicators**:
- Can announce scores without hesitation
- Uses Spanish phrases spontaneously during actual pickleball
- Asks to continue learning beyond 10 lessons
- Demonstrates vocabulary retention across sessions
