🧵 THREAD: When Trial Preparation Becomes Leverage

1/ "We're requesting a continuance."

It was the Friday before trial in Brevard County. The prosecutor wanted to push the case.

My response: "We oppose. Our witnesses are ready to appear."

Here's why that mattered...

2/ Most DUI defense is invisible.

Hours analyzing FDLE lab reports.
Witness coordination.
Cross-exam prep on DRE protocol.
Research on metabolite detection windows.

But the State doesn't see that work until you're actually ready for trial.

3/ In this case, we had:

✓ Multiple defense witnesses coordinated for trial week
✓ Cross-examination prepared on the DRE's training vs. experience
✓ Science breakdown on why metabolites ≠ impairment
✓ Alternative explanation for observations

We weren't bluffing. We were ready.

4/ Monday morning: calendar call.

30 cases on the docket.

State moves for continuance.
Defense opposes.

Judge looks at the file. Looks at the crowded docket. Looks at the State.

"Counsel, what's your position if the continuance is denied?"

5/ This is the moment where trial preparation becomes leverage.

When you're truly ready, the State has to make a choice:
- Prove the case NOW
- Or resolve it fairly

Because fairness isn't optional when the State is bringing charges.

6/ The science problem for the State:

Breath test: 0.000 (zero alcohol)
Urine test: Metabolites only (inactive substances, could be from days/weeks prior)
DRE: Only 1 year certified, limited real-world experience

How do you prove impairment with that?

7/ The Result:

DUI with property damage → Reckless driving (withhold)
Leaving the scene → Withhold
2nd DUI → Nolle prosequi (dropped)
2nd leaving scene → Dropped
3 traffic citations → Dismissed

Zero convictions. Zero jail time.

8/ The lesson:

Trial preparation isn't about intimidation.
It's about readiness.

When the State requests a continuance and you're truly ready — witnesses present, cross-exam prepared, theory solid — opposing that motion is powerful.

Because leverage comes from preparation, not posturing.

9/ A lot of defense work is invisible to the outside world.

But when you're ready for trial and the State knows it, that invisible work becomes very visible leverage.
