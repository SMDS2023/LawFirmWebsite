🧵 THREAD: Why Urine Tests Can't Prove DUI Impairment

1/ You failed a urine test. The State says you were impaired while driving.

But here's the science problem prosecutors don't want you to know...

2/ Urine tests detect METABOLITES — not active drugs.

What's a metabolite? It's what's LEFT OVER after your body processes a drug.

Think of it like ashes from a fire. The fire is out, but the ashes remain.

3/ THC Metabolite Example:

The State's lab found "11-Nor-9-carboxy-delta-9-THC" in the urine.

That's the INACTIVE metabolite of marijuana.

Detection window? 3-30 days AFTER use. Heavy users: up to 90 days.

4/ The Problem:

You could have used marijuana 2 weeks ago, completely sober while driving, and still test positive.

Metabolites ≠ Impairment
Metabolites = Prior Use

5/ Same Issue with Benzodiazepines:

- Alpha-hydroxyalprazolam (Xanax metabolite)
- Alpha-hydroxytriazolam (Halcion metabolite)

Both could be present from use DAYS before the alleged DUI.

6/ Blood Tests vs. Urine Tests:

Blood tests show what's ACTIVELY in your system at the time of the draw.

Urine tests show what WAS in your system at some point in the past.

That's why blood is the gold standard in DUI science.

Breath test? 0.000 (zero alcohol)
Urine test? Metabolites only
