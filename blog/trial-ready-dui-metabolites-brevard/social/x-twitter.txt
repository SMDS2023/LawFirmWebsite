🧵 THREAD: When Trial Readiness Forces a Favorable DUI Resolution

1/ Friday before trial: State requests continuance
Defense: "We oppose. Our witnesses are ready."

What happened next shows why preparation = leverage.

2/ The Case:
• 2 felonies (child neglect)
• 2 DUI charges
• 2 leaving the scene
• 3 traffic citations
MAX: 14 YEARS

The breath test? 0.000
State still prosecuted as drug DUI.

3/ The Science Problem for the State:

Urine test showed METABOLITES (not active drugs):
• THC metabolite: detectable 3-30 days AFTER use
• Xanax metabolite: prior use ≠ impairment
• Halcion metabolite: same issue

Metabolites don't prove you were impaired while driving.

4/ Monday morning calendar call:
• 30 cases on docket
• State moves for continuance
• Defense opposes (witnesses present & ready)

Trial readiness = leverage

5/ The Result:
✓ DUI → Reckless (withhold)
✓ Leaving scene → Withhold
✓ 2nd DUI → Dropped
✓ 2nd leaving scene → Dropped
✓ All traffic citations → Dismissed

NO convictions. NO jail. NO prison.

6/ The lesson:

A lot of defense work is invisible:
• Hours analyzing FDLE lab reports
• Witness coordination
• Research on metabolite detection windows
• DRE protocol preparation

But when you're ready for trial, the State has to make a choice.
