SLIDE 4: Blood vs. Urine
---
Heading: "BLOOD VS. URINE TESTS"

BLOOD TESTS:
✓ Show active drugs
✓ Reflect impairment NOW
✓ Gold standard

URINE TESTS:
❌ Show metabolites (inactive)
❌ Reflect prior use
❌ Can't prove impairment

CAPTION:
If you failed a urine test in a Florida DUI case, the science matters.

Urine tests detect METABOLITES — the inactive leftovers after your body processes a drug. They can stay in your system for days or even weeks after use.

Metabolites don't prove you were impaired while driving. They only prove you used a substance at some point in the past.

In our recent Brevard County case, the State relied on a urine test showing only metabolites. The breath test? 0.000 — zero alcohol.

We opposed their last-minute continuance, forced them to make a choice, and the case resolved: DUI reduced to reckless driving with no convictions.
