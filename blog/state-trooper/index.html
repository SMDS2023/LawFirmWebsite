Insider Knowledge Means Unmatched DUI Defense

Published on December 12, 2025

Jeff Lotter's Florida Highway Patrol Experience is a Critical Edge for Your Case

FHP DUI Squad | NHTSA DUI Instructor | MADD DUI Award Recipient

Get a Veteran State Trooper on Your Side

Florida Highway Patrol (2003 - 2009)

After his distinguished military service, Jeff Lotter transitioned to civilian law enforcement, graduating from the Florida Highway Patrol (FHP) State Trooper Academy in 2003. Assigned to Troop D in Orlando, he quickly distinguished himself, particularly in DUI enforcement. His years as a State Trooper provided him with an intimate understanding of traffic law, accident investigation, and the very tactics used by law enforcement in DUI and traffic cases—knowledge he now uses to fiercely defend his clients.

Specialized Roles & Expertise

  • FHP DUI Squad (Midnights): Volunteered for and served on the specialized DUI unit, focusing on identifying and apprehending impaired drivers, primarily on the west side of Orange County for five years.
  • NHTSA DUI SFST Course Graduate: Completed the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) Standardized Field Sobriety Testing (SFST) course, mastering the approved methods for DUI detection.
  • Drug Evaluation and Classification Program (DECP/DRE): Attended and gained expertise in identifying drug impairment, a critical skill in complex DUI cases.
  • NHTSA Certified DUI Instructor: Achieved certification to teach fellow officers the proper administration and interpretation of SFSTs, highlighting his expert-level understanding.
  • MADD 100 DUI Awards: Recognized by Mothers Against Drunk Driving (MADD) three times for his commitment to DUI enforcement, an award given for making over 100 DUI arrests in a year.
  • FHP Tactical Response Team (TRT): Selected for this elite unit, responsible for high-risk situations, civil disturbance, and dignitary protection.
  • Defensive Tactics Instructor & Police Instructor: Certified to teach defensive tactics and general police procedures, further solidifying his comprehensive knowledge.
  • Field Training Officer (FTO): Entrusted with training and mentoring new troopers, ensuring they met the high standards of the FHP.

The Trooper's Edge: How FHP Experience Defends You

Jeff Lotter's tenure as a Florida State Trooper, especially his focus on DUI enforcement and instructor certifications, offers an unparalleled advantage to his clients:

  • Expert Understanding of DUI Procedures: Having performed countless DUI investigations and taught the NHTSA SFST curriculum, Jeff knows exactly how these tests *should* be administered and where officers commonly make mistakes. He can meticulously scrutinize every step of your DUI stop.
  • Inside Knowledge of Police Tactics: As a former trooper, he understands the mindset, training, and common practices of arresting officers. This insight allows him to anticipate prosecution strategies and identify weaknesses in their case.
  • Proficiency in Traffic Law & Accident Investigation: His daily work involved enforcing traffic laws and investigating accidents, giving him a deep practical understanding of the nuances that can make or break a traffic-related case.
  • Credibility in Challenging Officer Testimony: When Jeff Lotter questions an officer's procedures or findings, he does so from a position of firsthand experience and certified expertise, which can be highly impactful.
  • Mastery of DRE Protocols: His training in the Drug Evaluation and Classification Program means he's adept at dissecting cases involving alleged drug impairment, often a complex area of DUI law.
  • Recognizing Procedural Errors: From the initial traffic stop to the breathalyzer or blood draw, Jeff knows the required protocols. Any deviation can be a powerful point for your defense.

If you're facing a DUI or serious traffic charge, having an attorney who has walked in the arresting officer's shoes, who knows their training manuals inside and out, and who has been recognized for his expertise in the field is an invaluable asset. Jeff Lotter brings that unique perspective to your defense.
