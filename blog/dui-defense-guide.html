Understanding DUI Defense in Florida

A DUI arrest in Florida triggers immediate consequences: license suspension, potential jail time, and a permanent criminal record. But an arrest is not a conviction. With the right defense strategy, many DUI cases can be reduced, dismissed, or won at trial.

Attorney Jeff Lotter brings a unique perspective to DUI defense. As a former Florida State Trooper and Deputy Sheriff, he knows exactly how DUI investigations are supposed to be conducted—and where officers frequently make mistakes that can help your case.
