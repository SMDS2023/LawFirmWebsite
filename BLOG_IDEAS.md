## 1. Intoxilyzer 8000 → 9000 Transition Series

**Status:** Research phase - need to locate 80-page FDLE certified records PDF

**Core Angle:** Florida's breath test machine runs on software last updated in 2012 (same year as original iPhone launch). Independent data analysis of FDLE's own certified records reveals physically impossible results.

### Key Points

- **Timeline:** Intoxilyzer 8000 launched 2007, software support ended 2012
- **COBRA database:** Data management problems from inception, "data bleed" between reports
- **FDLE defense:** "In a million+ tests, there will be outliers" - dismissive of systemic issues
- **Stuart Hyman:** Led defense bar litigation (1000s of hours invested)
- **Legislative response:** Laws modified to overcome defense strategies
- **Original research:** SQL/VBS analysis found impossible results in certified FDLE records
  - Example: Breath volume 0.00 with BAC reading of .119 - physically impossible

### Proposed Structure

**Post 1: The History**
- 8000's 2007 launch, software frozen since 2012
- Stuart Hyman and defense bar litigation efforts
- Legislature changing laws to protect the machine
- FDLE's "outliers" defense

**Post 2: The Data (Original Research)**
- SQL/VBS methodology for analyzing breath test records
- Specific impossible results (redacted case examples)
- What "data bleed" actually means
- FDLE's non-answers to technical questions

**Post 3: The 9000 Arrives (2026)**
- Will the same problems carry forward?
- Why the first year of new machine deployment matters
- What defendants should know during the transition
- Operator training/certification concerns

1. Blog series with embedded screenshots of certified records
2. Whitepaper/PDF download for attorney referrals
3. Consider FACDL newsletter submission
