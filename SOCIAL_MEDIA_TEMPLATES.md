## CONTENT BATCHING TIPS

### Monthly Content Batch Session (4-6 hours)

**Week 1: Planning**
- Review analytics from previous month
- Identify trending topics
- Plan month's blog posts
- Outline social content themes

**Week 2: Creation**
- Write blog posts (2-4)
- Create graphics (20-30)
- Record videos (2-4)
- Draft social captions (30-40)

**Week 3: Editing**
- Edit all written content
- Edit videos
- Finalize graphics
- Proofread everything

**Week 4: Scheduling**
- Schedule all social posts
- Set up email newsletters
- Upload videos with optimization
- Create content calendar for next month

### Tools for Batching
- **Scheduling:** Meta Business Suite (FB/IG), Hootsuite, Buffer, Later
- **Graphics:** Canva, Adobe Express
- **Video:** Adobe Premiere Pro, CapCut, iMovie
- **Writing:** Google Docs, Grammarly
- **Project Management:** Trello, Asana, Monday.com
