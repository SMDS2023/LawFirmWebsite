# RESEARCH — Build the Source Pack

Input: one approved brief from `BLOG_PIPELINE.md`. Output: a **Source Pack**
appended to that brief — everything WRITE needs so drafting never has to
guess or invent.

## Pull the actual text

For each authority cross-ref promised in the brief:

1. **Academy manual / instructor guide / OCSO policy** (Drive IDs in
   `DATA_SOURCES.md` §3): download to the scratchpad, locate the relevant
   pages, and extract short verbatim quotes with **page or section pinpoints**
   (and GO number + effective date for OCSO orders). Prefer Jeff's vetted
   Drive copies; fall back to ocso.com via WebFetch.
2. **Case law**: verify each case is in the VERIFIED library (LegalAIntel) or
   read the opinion PDF this session (Drive case-law folders, Google Scholar,
   Justia, CourtListener, Florida Law Weekly). Record: case name, citation,
   court, year, one-sentence holding *as the opinion states it*, and the
   library/R2 or source URL. Unverifiable ⇒ `[CITE NEEDED]`.
3. **Statutes**: quote element language from Online Sunshine, note
   subsection pinpoints (e.g., F.S. §316.1935(3)(a)).
4. **Original data** (intox8000, court-records datasets): note the specific
   figures to use and where they came from.

## Verify the story

The brief arrives Gate-0-confirmed by Jeff; research still cross-checks the
details against Gmail (sequence of events, what was offered, what the order
said, what procedure was actually followed) and fixes anything that differs.
Calendar entries prove scheduling only — outcomes live in email, Lawmatics,
or Jeff's answers. If email is inconclusive on a load-bearing fact, ask Jeff
rather than assume. Nothing from email is quoted or committed — it only
makes the story true.

## Source Pack format (append to the brief in BLOG_PIPELINE.md)

```markdown
#### Source Pack (researched YYYY-MM-DD)
- **Narrative timeline (anonymized):** 3-6 bullets, month-level.
- **Authorities:**
  - <source> · <pinpoint> · "short quote" · what it proves in the argument
  - Library: <Case v. State, cite (court year)> · VERIFIED · holding · URL
- **Statutes:** F.S. §___ — element language to quote.
- **Defense theory / thesis:** the single argument the post makes.
- **Internal links:** pillar + 2-3 supporting posts + practice-area page
  (from the gap check).
- **Open items:** [CITE NEEDED]s, facts needing Jeff's confirmation.
```

## Standards

- Every claim about training, policy, or case law in the eventual post must
  trace to a line in the Source Pack. If it's not in the pack, it doesn't go
  in the draft.
- Quotes stay short (a phrase or sentence) — we characterize, then quote the
  load-bearing words.
- If research kills the thesis (the policy doesn't say what we hoped), say so
  and return the brief to Jeff with a revised angle or a recommendation to
  drop it. A dead brief costs nothing; a wrong post costs credibility.
