2. **"Attorney" = "Abogado"** - Using "abogado" throughout. Is this the preferred term for your client base, or would "licenciado" be more appropriate?

3. **Florida acronyms** - Keeping DHSMV, HTO, CDL, BAC, SFST, etc. as English acronyms with Spanish explanations on first use. These are official Florida terms.

4. **Formal vs. informal tone** - Using "usted" (formal you) throughout, not "tu". This is standard for legal/professional content. Confirm?
